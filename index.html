DETECTION SIGNALS

What it checks

Urgent threats or pressure to act immediately
Requests for OTP, password, PIN, or bank details
Prize, refund, lottery, or unexpected reward claims
Shortened links, unusual URL patterns, or suspicious domain endings
Requests to install an app or pay a fee

QUICK RESPONSE

If a message looks suspicious

  • Do not click links or download attachments.
  • Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, or recovery codes.
  • Verify through an official app, website, or known phone number.
  • Do not call numbers provided only in the suspicious message.
  • Block and report the sender in the messaging app.
  • If money or sensitive information was shared, contact your bank or service provider immediately through official channels.
COMMON SCAM TYPES

Know the terms

Phishing: Fake messages or websites designed to trick people into sharing information.
Smishing: Phishing through SMS or messaging apps.
Vishing: Fraud attempts through voice calls.
Impersonation: Someone pretends to be a trusted person or organization.
Payment scam: A false request to send money or approve a transaction.
PROJECT OVERVIEW

About Cyber Shield

Cyber Shield is a student project concept designed to help users recognize common cyber-fraud warning signs, understand why a message may be suspicious, and learn safer response steps.

Current prototype: Local rule-based text analysis, URL-pattern checks, incident form, local dashboard, sample messages, and safety guide.
Future development: Verified threat-intelligence API, secure backend, privacy controls, official reporting integration, multilingual support, and expert-reviewed detection.
Educational prototype only. It is not a professional security tool and cannot guarantee a message or URL is safe.
